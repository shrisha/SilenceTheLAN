# SilenceTheLAN Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2026

SilenceTheLAN is a local-network utility app for managing existing UniFi firewall rules.

## Data Collection

SilenceTheLAN does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the developer.

The app does not use:
- Analytics SDKs
- Advertising SDKs
- Third-party tracking

## Credentials and Local Data

To connect to your UniFi Controller, the app uses credentials you enter.

- Credentials and session tokens are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage.
- Selected rule configuration is stored locally on your device.
- This data is used only to operate the app's core functionality.

## Network Communication

SilenceTheLAN communicates directly with your UniFi Controller on your local network.

- No cloud relay service is used by SilenceTheLAN.
- The developer does not receive your controller data or credentials.

## Children

SilenceTheLAN is intended for parents/guardians and is not directed to children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated in future versions. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
